Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Mushroom Atlas (mushroomatlas.eu) is a free European mushroom guide with an optional account for sharing finds. This page explains what we collect and why.

Without an account

You can browse the atlas without signing in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your language choice is kept in your own browser. Our host (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site securely.

With an account

If you sign in with Google or an email link, we store your email address, display name and profile picture (from Google, if you use it). This is used only to run your account.

Finds and photos

When you add a find, we store the photo, the species, your note and the location you chose. Photos are re-encoded in your browser before upload, which removes hidden camera and GPS data. On the public map, locations are rounded to roughly 1 km unless you choose to show the exact spot, and finds marked private are visible only to you.

Photo identification

If you use photo identification, the photo is sent to our identification provider (Kindwise, mushroom.id) to produce suggestions. We keep a count of your identifications to apply plan limits.

Where data is stored

Account data, finds and photos are stored with Supabase in the EU (Ireland). We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, including your whole account. Write to info@mushroomatlas.eu and we will respond within 30 days.
